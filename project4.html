Industry Night Website Hero Video

Industry Night Website Hero Video

About The Project

This project is a promotional hero video created for Fanshawe College's Industry Night event. The video now is being displayed on our Industry Night Website. I worked as part of a team and was responsible for developing an energetic and engaging video that highlights the event and supports the Fanshawe brand identity.

The objective was to create a short, eye-catching piece that communicates excitement, professionalism, and creativity. The editing style focuses on fast pacing, dynamic cuts, and music-driven transitions to match the atmosphere of the event.

The video was edited in Adobe Premiere Pro, where I handled sequencing, timing, transitions, color adjustments, and audio synchronization. The final result functions as a hero section asset for the Industry Night website, designed to immediately capture attention when users land on the page.

Challenges

One challenge was balancing fast-paced editing with clear communication. The video needed to feel energetic without overwhelming the viewer.

Another challenge was maintaining alignment with the Fanshawe brand identity, ensuring the color grading, typography, and pacing matched the overall tone of the event.

Integrating the video into the website hero section also required attention to loading behavior and responsiveness.
