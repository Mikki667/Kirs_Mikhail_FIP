About me

Hello everyone! My name is Mikhail Kirs, and I'm a designer and web developer from Saint Petersburg, Russia. In April 2026, I graduated from the Interactive Media Design program at Fanshawe College in London, Canada.

I consider myself a skilled and reliable specialist in both design and coding.

In addition to web development, I have experience in video editing and filming, along with basic skills in 3D modeling using Cinema 4D. If your company is looking for a creative, motivated, and fresh-minded professional, I'd be excited to bring my talents to your team.
