Kovorka Makeup

About The Project

I was tasked with taking a name of the non-existing brand and turning it into the makeup line with the full brand guide and the promotional video. KAVORKA is a modern luxury cosmetics brand built around the concept of magnetic allure.Its name, derived from a Slavic term meaning “the lure,” symbolizes irresistible confidence and charm.The brand represents the quiet strength and sophistication of individuals who command attention effortlessly. KAVORKA’s design language blends minimalist geometry with subtle elegance.The aesthetic combines clean black and gold contrasts, soft rose tones, and refined typography to create an atmosphere of timeless beauty and power.

The design elements were built from scratch using Adobe Illustrator for the logo and color palette. Figma was used for the style guide and also the adobe After Effects for the video
